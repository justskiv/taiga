{{- /* The guide's RUBRIC — the shelf a reader can actually open — or false when it has none. Context = a guide page. Returns the section PAGE, so callers take the label off it themselves (params.label | lower title). Guides live in two kinds of section (guide-sections.html). A RUBRIC is one of params.rubricSections: it has a landing page, a card on the home page and a place in the nav, so naming it hands the reader somewhere to go. The sections of params.extraGuideSections are the other kind — a catch-all ("misc", "разное") that carries loose pieces into the feed, search and RSS without ever being a shelf; its _index.md is typically `render: never`, so .RelPermalink on it is the empty string and the kicker link the templates used to draw pointed at nothing at all. Hence false rather than the section page: a name with no destination is worse than no name, and the reader loses nothing — the line still says what the piece is ("deep dive", or the series and part).
