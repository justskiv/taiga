{{- /* A date as markup: "27 Jun" with the year in a span of its own, 27 Jun 2026. Context = a time value. The year is ALWAYS written and hidden on the reader's side when it is the reader's own year (assets/js/prefs.js). date.html decides the same thing against `now` — the day of the BUILD — so a page built in December still drops last year's year in January, until someone rebuilds the site. Without JS the year simply stays: longer, never wrong. Month names are the same i18n keys date.html reads (month_1…month_12). */ -}} {{- $mon := i18n (printf "month_%d" (int .Month)) -}} {{- $html := printf `%d %s %d` (.Format "2006-01-02") .Day $mon .Year .Year -}} {{- return safeHTML $html -}}
