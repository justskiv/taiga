{{- /* Automatic end-of-article block (placements.articleEnd), rendered from page.html after the series bridge. Off by default: dropping a form into the foot of every guide on a site is a decision its owner makes once, not something a theme does on installation. Skipped when the page already carries a hand-written {{< newsletter-cta >}} — the shortcode raises a flag on the page store as the content renders, and this partial runs after it. Without that check an author who wrote their own CTA (the whole point of the per-article texts) would get a second, generic one underneath. A mid-text {{< newsletter >}} block does NOT suppress it: those two are different offers in different places, and an article may carry both. Texts: front matter `newsletterCta: {title, body, note}` overrides the i18n defaults — the same three keys the shortcode takes, so a series can set its wording once in the section cascade. */ -}} {{- $nl := partialCached "newsletter/cfg.html" . .Language.Lang -}} {{- if and $nl.enable $nl.placements.articleEnd (not (.Store.Get "nlCta")) -}} {{- .Store.Set "nlForm" true -}} {{- $fm := .Params.newsletterCta | default dict -}} {{- $body := "" -}} {{- with $fm.body }}{{ $body = $.RenderString (dict "display" "block") . }}{{ end -}} {{ partial "newsletter/cta.html" (dict "title" ($fm.title | default "") "body" $body "note" ($fm.note | default "")) }} {{- end -}}
